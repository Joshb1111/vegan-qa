# Far Field: visual target (from Josh, 7 Oct 2026)

`visual-target.webp` (1774x887, four panels) is the look Josh wants: simple 3D environments with side-on gameplay, smooth matte surfaces, restrained colours, atmospheric depth and strong directional lighting. It is a mood target, not art to copy into the game.

1. Top left: a vast dark interior. A tall concrete wall with a small louvred vent high up; diagonal light shafts from the upper left through haze; a broad pool of pale light on the floor where a small pale rabbit sits upright in profile. A leaning metal grate in the left foreground, a raised structure with two cylindrical tanks mid-distance on the right, planks on the floor, a cable hanging from a dark box at the top right.
2. Top right: a big pipe elbow overhead; the rabbit head-pushes a wooden crate towards a small, low, glowing opening in the wall; on the right a dark pool of water with a wooden walkway, a valve wheel, and a raised platform with a railing and one small amber light (the only warm accent).
3. Bottom left: a yard at night. The rabbit crouches in shadow under a low concrete slab on the left; a human silhouette (cap, backpack) walks on the right, with a torch beam cutting a cone across the floor towards the rabbit.
4. Bottom right: overcast dawn. The rabbit steps out of a concrete culvert onto grass with tall weed silhouettes; a huge sloping wall behind; a misty distant landscape with a chimney stack; a pale grey sky.

Common to all: camera side-on and low, so ordinary objects are huge and the rabbit is small in the frame; near-black charcoal and cold blue-grey haze, low saturation; light as the main design tool (shafts, pools, beams); the pale rabbit reads by value against dark surroundings; untextured, matte surfaces with soft shadows and depth fog.
