Tyrian in the planet game's arcade

This is OpenTyrian v2.1 (https://github.com/opentyrian/opentyrian), free software under the GNU General Public
License version 2, compiled to WebAssembly with Emscripten. Changes from upstream:
  - SDL_net is replaced by SDL_net.h and sdlnet_web.c (both in this folder): packets go to and from the page,
    which carries them between the two players over a WebRTC data channel or the Ably room.
  - src/tyrian2.c: a network game does not pause when the browser window loses focus.
The game data is Tyrian 2.1, released as freeware by Jason Emery in 2004: "Feel free to play it all you want and
share it with friends."
